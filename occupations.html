AI Exposure Across 923 Occupations

Browse exposure scores from four sources: OpenAI AI-rated & human-rated tiers, Brynjolfsson et al. SML scores, and Anthropic behavioral data (task success, AI autonomy, work use). Switch sources using the dropdown.

Key findings
  • Knowledge work scores highest. Roles built on writing, math and analysis lead AI exposure; physical, manual and in-person service roles trail near zero.
  • Average exposure is ~34%. Across all 923 occupations, about a third of tasks are theoretically AI-performable (GAIA-E, Eloundou et al. 2024).
  • Higher exposure ≠ lower pay. In the wage chart below, the most-exposed jobs are often the higher-paid ones — exposure tracks cognitive work, not low wages.
  • Four lenses, kept separate. Switch sources to compare AI-rated, human-rated, the pre-GPT SML baseline (GAIA-B), and Anthropic's observed usage.
