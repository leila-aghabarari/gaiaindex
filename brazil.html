Brazil: work-first AI adoption

Brazil uses Claude.ai for work more than almost anywhere else. This page compares Brazil with its peers and traces how AI exposure can transmit through the banking system into jobs — the focus of ongoing work with Banco Central do Brasil.

How exposure reaches jobs

In the Brazil study, occupational AI exposure doesn't hit employment directly — it travels through the credit channel: exposed firms face tighter bank lending, which feeds through to hiring and wages.

Source: “AI Exposure, Credit Markets & Employment in Brazil” (forthcoming, with Banco Central do Brasil). See the working paper.
