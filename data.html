GAIA Occupations
Merged dataset combining OpenAI exposure ratings, Brynjolfsson SML scores, and Anthropic AEI behavioral data across 923 O*NET-SOC occupations. Includes GAIA-E (generative-AI exposure, n=923) and GAIA-B (supervised-ML exposure, n=605) as separate normalized scores.
 923 occupations GAIA-E · GAIA-B · Alpha/Beta/Gamma · SML score · AEI task success 2023–2026

Data access by request. Cite: Aghabarari (2026)
OpenAI Occupational Exposure → GAIA-E
AI and human expert ratings of task-level AI exposure (Eloundou et al., Science 2024). Alpha = fully automatable, Beta = partially (with tools), Gamma = any exposure. Normalized to [0,1] as GAIA-E in the occupations file.
 923 occupations dv_alpha, dv_beta, dv_gamma, h_alpha, h_beta, h_gamma · gaia_e (0–1) 2023 Included in occupations CSV
SML — What Can Machines Learn? → GAIA-B
Crowd-sourced susceptibility-to-machine-learning scores at the DWA task level, aggregated to occupations. Brynjolfsson, Mitchell & Rock (2018), NBER Working Paper 24196. Normalized to [0,1] as GAIA-B in the occupations file.
 605 occupations sml_score (0–100%) · gaia_b (0–1) 2018 Included in occupations CSV
Anthropic Economic Index (AEI)
Behavioral data from Claude.ai API showing which O*NET tasks AI is being used for. Metrics: task success rate, AI autonomy level, share of work tasks vs. personal/learning. Released Feb 2026.
 628 occupations aei_task_success, aei_autonomy_pct, aei_work_pct Feb 2026 Included in occupations CSV

GAIA Countries (composite)
Country-level dataset with Anthropic usage patterns, IMF AIPI scores, ISO codes, and income classifications for 138 countries.
 138 countries usage_pct_global, uc_work, ts_yes, aipi_overall 2023–2026

Data access by request. Cite: Aghabarari (2026)
IMF AI Preparedness Index (AIPI)
Composite index measuring country readiness for AI adoption across four pillars: digital infrastructure, innovation & economic integration, human capital & labor market policies, and AI regulation.
 174 countries aipi_overall, aipi_digital_infra, aipi_innovation, aipi_human_capital, aipi_regulation 2023

Data access by request. Cite: Aghabarari (2026)
Gallup World Poll — AI Sentiment
Public attitudes toward AI: share who believe AI will mostly help vs. mostly harm society in the next 20 years. Sourced from Our World in Data (Gallup World Poll 2021).
 119 countries ai_mostly_help, ai_mostly_harm, ai_net_optimism 2021

Data access by request. Cite: Aghabarari (2026)
OECD.AI — Policy Initiatives
Total number of national AI policy initiatives per country tracked by the OECD AI Policy Observatory. Includes strategies, legislative acts, regulatory frameworks, and policy recommendations.
 138 countries ai_policies, has_strategy (0/1), has_law (0/1) 2024

Data access by request. Cite: Aghabarari (2026)
Eurostat ICT Survey — Firm AI Adoption
Share of enterprises with 10 or more employees that use AI technologies (e.g. machine learning, NLP, computer vision, robots). Eurostat dataset isoc_eb_ai, indicator E_AI_TANY.
 33 European countries ai_adoption_latest, ai_adoption_2024, ai_adoption_2023, ai_adoption_2021 2024

Data access by request. Cite: Aghabarari (2026)
